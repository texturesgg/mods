# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Take over: X pauses the replay and asks who plays each player from here (the recording, nobody, or one of your controllers). Then it's a normal match; D-pad left puts you back to that moment to try again.
- D-pad left goes back about 2 seconds while watching; D-pad right skips about 10 seconds ahead.
- A replay plays with everything unlocked, as netplay does; your own save keeps what it had.
